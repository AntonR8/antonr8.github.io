Common questions

Where do the figures come from?

Limit percentages come from your own claude.ai account, read with the session you sign in to inside the App. On Mac, money amounts are computed from the log files Claude Code writes on your computer, using a built-in price table. Both are informational: Anthropic's own account pages are the authority on your limits and your bill.

My iPhone or Apple Watch shows old numbers

Figures gathered on your Mac reach your other devices through your own private iCloud storage. Check that the Mac is awake and online, and that iCloud is on for the App on both devices.

Is Burnrate made by Anthropic?

No. Burnrate is an independent app, not affiliated with or endorsed by Anthropic.

Where is my data kept?

On your devices and in your own private iCloud storage. We run no servers and never receive it. See the Privacy Policy for the details.
