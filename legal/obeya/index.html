Obeya

Project planning and reflection for iOS

Obeya runs entirely on your device. There is no account and no server of ours, so we cannot see your projects — and cannot restore them. Export your data regularly; the app writes an open JSON file you can keep anywhere.

Common questions

My projects disappeared, or I switched devices. Data lives on your device only and we hold no copy. Restoring a device from an iCloud or computer backup restores the app's data with it. To move projects deliberately, use export to JSON on the old device and import the file on the new one.

I paid, but the paid tools are locked. Make sure the device is signed in to the same Apple ID used for the purchase, then use Restore purchases in the app.

How do I cancel a subscription. In Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device. Cancelling takes effect at the end of the paid period, and cancelling less than 24 hours before it ends does not stop that renewal. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Deadline reminders do not arrive. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Check Settings → Notifications → Obeya, and make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb is not suppressing them.

How do I delete everything. Delete individual items in the app, or delete the app from the device — that removes its local database entirely. A device backup may still hold an older copy under Apple's control.
