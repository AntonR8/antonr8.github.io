Privacy Policy — Obeya

Last updated: 4 August 2026

Obeya does not collect your personal data. There is no account, no sign-up, and no server operated by us. Everything you create in the app — projects, work breakdown structures, milestones, tasks, worksheets, self-assessments and journal entries — is stored only on your own device.

We have no technical ability to read your project data. There is no analytics, no advertising, no tracking, and no third-party SDK in the app.

This policy explains that in full.

2. Data stored on your device

The app stores the following locally, inside its own sandboxed storage area on your device:

  • projects, their work breakdown structure, milestones, dates and ordering;
  • tasks, statuses, deadlines and filters;
  • worksheets produced by the tools, including your written answers and self-assessment scores;
  • journal entries and lessons you record;
  • app settings, such as the selected project, colour and notification preference.

This information never leaves your device unless you deliberately export it (see section 5) or unless it is included in a backup you have configured with Apple (see section 6).

We do not receive this data. We cannot access it, restore it, or recover it for you.

3. Data we do not collect

We want to be specific rather than vague. The app contains:

  • no user accounts, registration, login or email collection;
  • no servers, APIs or network requests operated by us — the app makes no network calls of its own;
  • no analytics or telemetry of any kind (no Firebase, no Amplitude, no crash-reporting SDK);
  • no advertising, ad networks, or advertising identifiers (IDFA);
  • no tracking of you across apps or websites, and therefore no App Tracking Transparency prompt;
  • no third-party software development kits;
  • no location collection;
  • no contacts, calendar, microphone or camera access;
  • no sale or sharing of personal information, because none is collected.

Accordingly, the app's App Store privacy label is declared as Data Not Collected.

4. Notifications

If you enable deadline reminders, the app asks iOS for notification permission and schedules reminders locally on your device. No push server is involved and no notification content is transmitted anywhere. You can withdraw the permission at any time in iOS Settings → Notifications, or turn reminders off inside the app.

5. Export and import

The app lets you export your projects as a JSON file and import such a file back.

  • Export is always started by you. The file is handed to the standard iOS share sheet, and you choose the destination — Files, Mail, a messenger, a cloud service, or another app.
  • Once you send the file somewhere, that destination's own privacy policy governs it. We have no involvement in and no control over what happens to the file after it leaves the app.
  • Import reads a JSON file that you select. The file is parsed on your device only.

Treat exported files as you would any document containing your work: they are readable plain text.

6. Backups

If you have enabled iCloud Backup or you back up your device to a computer, Apple's backup may include the app's local database. Those backups are created and controlled by Apple under Apple's Privacy Policy, not by us. You can exclude the app from iCloud Backup in iOS Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Backups.

7. Purchases

Subscriptions are sold and processed by Apple through the App Store. We never see or receive your payment card details, billing address, or Apple ID.

Apple provides us with aggregated, anonymised sales and subscription reports in App Store Connect. These show totals — units, revenue, country, retention — and do not identify individual users. Apple's handling of your purchase data is governed by Apple's own privacy policy and the App Store terms.

Subscription status is validated on your device through Apple's StoreKit framework.

8. Children

The app is intended for adults working on professional or personal projects. It is not directed at children, and we do not knowingly collect data from anyone — including children — because we do not collect data at all.

9. Your rights

Data-protection laws — the Law of the Republic of Armenia on Protection of Personal Data, the EU GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA/CPRA and Russian Federal Law No. 152-FZ — grant you rights over personal data held about you: access, correction, erasure, portability, restriction and objection.

Because we hold no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to disclose, correct or erase on our side. In practice:

  • Access and portability — use the export function; it gives you your complete data in an open, machine-readable JSON format.
  • Erasure — delete individual items in the app, or delete the app from your device, which removes its local database entirely. Note that this is irreversible and that a device backup may still hold an older copy under Apple's control.

If you nevertheless wish to exercise a right or ask a question, write to antonr8.apps@outlook.com and we will respond within the period required by the applicable law.

10. Retention

Your data remains on your device for as long as you keep it there. We do not retain anything, because we never receive anything. Deleting the app deletes its stored data.

11. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your own device: the iOS application sandbox, and device passcode and file-system encryption where you have enabled them. We recommend keeping a device passcode enabled. Since no data is transmitted to us, there is no transmission for us to secure and no server of ours that could be breached.

If the app gains a feature that changes any of the above — for example optional cloud synchronisation — we will update this policy before that feature ships and change the date at the top. Material changes will also be described in the App Store release notes.
