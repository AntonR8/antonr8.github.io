Plentia

Gardening companion for iPhone, Apple Watch and widgets

Plentia works on your device. We run no servers and no accounts, so we cannot see your gardens, plants or photos — which also means we cannot recover them for you.

Common questions

Weather or my climate zone is wrong or missing. Plentia needs location access "While Using the App" — check Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services → Plentia. Forecasts come from Apple Weather and can be delayed or unavailable in some regions. The app stays usable without location, with less personalization.

Reminders do not arrive. Reminders are local notifications scheduled on your device. Check Settings → Notifications → Plentia, and make sure Focus or Do Not Disturb is not suppressing them.

My data disappeared. Garden data lives on your device only. We hold no copy and cannot restore it. If you keep an iCloud device backup or a computer backup, restoring the device restores the app's data with it.

Widget or Apple Watch shows nothing. Both read a small "today's tasks" snapshot from the iPhone. Open the app on the iPhone once, then check that the Watch is paired and in range.
