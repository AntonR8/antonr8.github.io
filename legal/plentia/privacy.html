Privacy Policy for Plentia

Last updated: 29 July 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Plentia ("Plentia", the "App", "we", "us") handles information when you use the App. Plentia is a gardening companion that helps you plan and track the care of your plants, beds, and greenhouse.

The App is provided by Anton Razgulaev, Individual Entrepreneur ("we"), registered in the Republic of Armenia (state registration number 286.1580989, taxpayer ID (TIN) 20286933).

Short version: Plentia is built to work almost entirely on your device. We do not operate accounts or our own servers, we do not run analytics or advertising, and we do not sell or share your data. Your garden data stays on your device. The only information that leaves your device is your approximate location, which is sent to Apple to provide weather and climate-zone features, and any purchases, which are handled by Apple.

1. Who this policy applies to

This policy applies to everyone who uses the Plentia app for iPhone, its widgets, and the companion Apple Watch app.

2. Information we do NOT collect

To be clear about what Plentia does not do, we do not:

  • Require you to create an account, sign in, or provide your name, email, or phone number to use the App.
  • Operate our own backend servers that store your personal data.
  • Use third-party analytics, tracking, or advertising SDKs (no Firebase, no crash-reporting SDKs, no ad networks).
  • Track you across apps or websites, or build advertising profiles.
  • Sell, rent, or share your personal information with data brokers.
  • Send your photos to any server or AI/cloud service.

3. Information stored on your device

The App stores the following information locally on your device (using Apple's on-device storage and a shared app group container). This data is under your control and is removed when you delete the App:

  • Your garden content — gardens, plants, care tasks, harvest and diagnostic notes, and any garden map objects you create.
  • Photos you attach — pictures you choose to add to a plant or a task from your photo library. These are saved only in the App's private storage on your device and are never uploaded. Plentia does not use the camera and does not identify or analyze your photos on any server.
  • App preferences — settings such as appearance, onboarding completion, notification preferences, and your premium/purchase status flag.
  • "Today" snapshot — a small summary of today's tasks that is shared, on your device only, with the Plentia widget and Apple Watch app through an Apple App Group.

We do not have access to any of this on-device data.

4. Location and weather

If you grant permission, Plentia uses your device location ("While Using the App" only) to:

  • Determine your climate/hardiness zone and region to tailor your plant-care plan, and
  • Show local weather relevant to plant care.

How location is handled:

  • Location is requested only while you are using the App. Plentia does not use background or "Always" location.
  • Region detection uses Apple's on-device/reverse-geocoding services.
  • To provide the forecast, your location coordinates are sent to Apple's WeatherKit service. This transfer is governed by Apple's privacy terms; we do not receive or store your location on any server of ours.
  • You can decline or revoke location access at any time in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Location Services. The App remains usable without location, with reduced weather/climate personalization.

Weather data is provided by Apple Weather. Learn more at Apple's Weather and privacy resources.

5. Notifications

Plentia can send local notifications (scheduled on your device) to remind you about upcoming plant-care tasks. These reminders are generated on-device; the App does not use remote/push notifications and does not send notification content to any server. You can enable or disable notifications at any time in iOS Settings or within the App.

6. Apple Watch and widgets

The Plentia widget and Apple Watch app read a small "today's tasks" snapshot shared on your device via an Apple App Group. The iPhone and Apple Watch communicate directly with each other using Apple's on-device connectivity; this data does not pass through our servers.

7. Purchases and subscriptions

Plentia may offer optional premium features through in-app purchases or subscriptions. All purchases are processed by Apple through the App Store using your Apple ID. We do not receive or store your payment card details or billing information — Apple handles the transaction and only provides us with the information needed to confirm your purchase status. Subscriptions, billing, and refunds are managed through your Apple ID account settings and are subject to Apple's terms.

8. Links to other services

The App may include links (for example, weather attribution, our Terms of Use, and this Privacy Policy) that open in your browser. Once you leave the App, the privacy practices of the destination website apply. This policy does not cover third-party websites.

9. Children's privacy

Plentia is a general-audience app and is not directed to children under the age of 16 (or the minimum age required in your country). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. Because the App does not collect personal data on our servers, no such information is transmitted to us.

10. Data retention and deletion

Your data lives on your device for as long as you keep the App installed. You can delete individual items within the App, and you can remove all app data by deleting the App from your device. Because we do not store your personal data on our servers, there is no separate server-side deletion request needed.

11. Security

Your data is protected by the security features of your Apple device and operating system, including device-level encryption when enabled. Because your data stays on your device and is not sent to our servers, the primary safeguard is the security of your own device (passcode, Face ID/Touch ID, and iOS protections).

12. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights under laws such as the EU/UK GDPR or the California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), including rights to access, correct, or delete your personal information. Because Plentia stores your data on your own device and we do not hold it on servers, you can exercise these rights directly by viewing, editing, or deleting your data within the App or by deleting the App. If you have questions, contact us using the details below.

13. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date above and post the new version. Significant changes will be reflected in the App or on our website.
