What Ciclia is, and what it is not

Ciclia is a contraception and cycle tracker. It records what you enter, keeps to the schedule of the method you chose, reminds you, and explains what published guidance says.

Ciclia is not a medical device and does not give medical advice. It does not diagnose, does not treat, and does not tell you whether you are safe. It gives no percentages of effectiveness and no verdict about your personal risk, deliberately. Predictions about your cycle are estimates from your own history and can be wrong.

Ciclia is not a method of contraception. It does not prevent pregnancy. Only the method you actually use does that, and the app cannot know whether you used it correctly.

The reference articles in the app are written from public guidance — the NHS, the CDC, the FSRH and equivalent bodies — and they say where a source is silent. They are general information, not advice about you. Decisions about a method, a symptom or a medicine belong to you and to whoever prescribes for you.
