What stays on your device

Everything you enter — your method and its schedule, the days you marked, bleeding, discharge, symptoms, mood, sex, and your notes — is stored on your device. It is not sent to us, and we have no server that could receive it. We cannot read it, and neither can anyone else who does not have your device or your iCloud account.

iCloud

If iCloud is enabled on your device, your entries sync through your own private iCloud database, so the app works across your iPhone, iPad and Apple Watch. This happens between you and Apple. Your data is not visible to us at any point, and it lives under your Apple Account, subject to Apple's privacy policy. Turning iCloud off keeps everything on the one device.

Apple Health

Ciclia can write your cycle entries into the Cycle Tracking section of Apple Health. This is optional and off until you turn it on. Ciclia does not read your health data. Health data is governed by Apple and never passes through us.

Purchases

Ciclia Plus is an optional subscription. Purchases are processed by Apple; we never see your payment details. To know whether your subscription is active, the app uses Adapty, a subscription-management service, which receives purchase history only — which product was bought, and whether it is still valid. Adapty receives none of your cycle, health or diary data. That information is not linked to your identity and is not used for tracking or advertising.

What we do not do

  • There is no advertising in Ciclia, and no advertising SDK in the app.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and we do not ask for permission to, because we have no use for it.
  • We do not build an analytics profile of you and do not run third-party analytics.
  • We do not sell, rent or share your data. There is nothing to sell: we do not have it.
  • Ciclia has no accounts. You do not register, and we hold no email address or password of yours.

Locking the app

You can require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode before Ciclia opens, and the app hides its content in the app switcher either way. This check happens on your device; your biometric data never leaves it and is never available to us.

Your data is yours

You can export everything to a CSV file, or a one-page PDF summary, and send it wherever you choose. Deleting an entry removes it. Deleting the app, and clearing its iCloud data from your Apple Account settings, removes everything. Because we never receive your entries, there is no copy on our side to request or erase — a right you hold under the GDPR, the UK GDPR, the CCPA and similar laws is satisfied by the fact that the data never left you.

Children

Ciclia is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Not medical advice

Ciclia is not a medical device. It does not provide medical advice, diagnosis or treatment, does not detect pregnancy, and is not itself a method of contraception. Timings come from the leaflet supplied with your own method. Follow that leaflet, and speak to your doctor or pharmacist.

Changes

If this policy changes, the date at the top changes with it, and a material change will be announced in the app before it takes effect.
