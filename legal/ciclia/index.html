Common questions

My reminder did not arrive

Check that notifications are allowed: Settings → Ciclia → Notifications on your device. Then open Ciclia's own settings and confirm the reminder time is the one you want. If Focus or Do Not Disturb is on at that hour, iOS holds the notification back — you can allow Ciclia through in Settings → Focus.

Can I change the reminder time?

Yes, any time, and it costs nothing. Open settings and pick the hour that suits you. You can also add a second reminder later the same day if you tend to swipe notifications away, and an alert three days before your pack runs out. All of that is free.

I forgot to mark a day

Open the calendar, tap the day, and fill it in. Editing past days is free and always will be. Your protection status and your chart update from the corrected history.

I am switching methods

Open settings and choose Change method. Your calendar, chart and every note stay exactly where they are — the switch becomes part of your history instead of starting you over.

Where is my data kept?

On your device, and — if iCloud is on — in your own private iCloud database. We never receive it. See the privacy policy for the details.

How do I get my data out?

Settings offers a CSV export of everything, and Ciclia Plus adds a one-page PDF summary written for a doctor's appointment.

Subscriptions and refunds

I bought Plus but it is not showing. Open the paywall and tap Restore purchases. Make sure the device is signed in to the Apple Account that made the purchase. Subscriptions cover all your devices signed in to the same account.

How do I cancel Ciclia Plus? Subscriptions are managed by Apple, not by us: Settings → your name → Subscriptions on your device, or from Ciclia's settings via Manage subscription. Cancelling stops the next renewal; you keep access until the period you have paid for ends. Deleting the app does not cancel a subscription.

Refunds are handled by Apple at reportaproblem.apple.com, not by us.

Not medical advice

Ciclia is not a medical device and does not give medical advice, diagnosis or treatment. It does not detect pregnancy and is not a method of contraception on its own. Timings come from the leaflet supplied with your own method — follow it, and speak to your doctor or pharmacist.
