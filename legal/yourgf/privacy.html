Privacy Policy — Your GF App

Effective date: 4 August 2026

Last updated: 4 August 2026

The short version

Your GF App keeps everything on your phone.

We do not run servers. We do not have accounts. We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking. The name and cycle dates you enter never leave your device, and we never see them — we could not retrieve them even if we wanted to.

You can erase everything at any time from Settings → Erase All Data.

2. Scope

This policy covers the Your GF App iOS application and its home-screen widget. It does not cover Apple's own services (the App Store, subscription billing), which are governed by Apple's Privacy Policy.

3. What the app stores on your device

The app stores the following on your iPhone, in the app's private storage and a shared container used by the widget:

What Why
The name you enter for your partnerShown as the title of the main screen
Cycle start date, cycle end date and cycle lengthUsed to estimate the current cycle phase
Compliments you write or saveShown back to you in the Compliments tab
Tips you marked as used, tried, or favouriteSo the app remembers your choices
Your ratings of tipsShown back to you inside the app
A yes/no flag for whether a subscription is activeSo the widget knows what to show
Dates you choose to remember, and what each one is (for example "her mother's birthday")So the app can remind you in advance
Which reminders you switched onSo the app knows what to schedule
App settings (onboarding completed, consent acknowledgement, app lock on or off)So the app behaves consistently

That is the complete list.

We store the *type* of a date, never a person's name: "her mother's birthday", not who her mother is. The app has no access to your contacts and cannot import anything from them.

We do not store: photographs, contacts, precise or approximate location, device identifiers, advertising identifiers, e-mail addresses, phone numbers, health records from Apple Health, or anything else.

The app does not request access to your photo library, camera, contacts, location, microphone, or Apple Health.

Reminders. The app can send you reminders — a phase change, a date to remember, a weekly tip. These are *local* notifications: your iPhone schedules and shows them itself, from the dates already on the device. Nothing is sent to us, and there is no push server involved. Permission is only requested at the moment you switch a reminder on, and you can turn them all off again in Settings → Notifications.

4. What we collect

Nothing.

Apple defines "collect" as transmitting data off the device in a way that lets the developer or its partners access it. By that definition — and by any other reasonable one — we collect no data.

The app contains no networking code. It makes no requests to us or to any third party. There is no account system, no cloud sync, no backup to our systems, no crash reporting, no analytics SDK, and no advertising SDK. There are no third-party SDKs in the app at all.

Accordingly, our App Store privacy label states "Data Not Collected".

5. Information about another person

This is the most important section of this policy, so please read it carefully.

Your GF App is designed for you to enter information — including menstrual cycle dates, which is health information — about another person. That person is not the one using the app, and cannot agree to anything through it.

Your responsibilities. You may only use this app if the person whose cycle you are recording knows that you are using it and has agreed to it. The app asks you to confirm this before you enter anything. Using this app to monitor someone without their knowledge is a misuse of it, is prohibited by our Terms of Use, and may be unlawful where you live.

Who is responsible for that data. Because the information stays on your device and never reaches us, you — not us — decide what is entered and what happens to it. Under data protection law you are generally the controller of that information, and in most jurisdictions a person recording information about a partner for purely personal purposes falls within the "personal or household activity" exemption. We provide the tool; we do not process the information.

If you are the person being recorded. If someone has entered your information into this app without your agreement, we cannot delete it for you — we do not have it, and we have no way to identify or reach the device it is on. The data can only be removed on that device, through Settings → Erase All Data, or by deleting the app. If you want to raise a concern with us anyway, write to antonr8.apps@outlook.com and we will help however we can.

6. Subscriptions

The app offers optional auto-renewable subscriptions. Payment is handled entirely by Apple. We never see your name, payment card, billing address, or Apple ID.

The app asks Apple whether a valid subscription exists and stores only the answer — a single yes/no value — on your device, so the widget knows whether to show content. That value contains no personal information.

7. How long data is kept, and how to delete it

Everything stays on your device until you remove it. There is no expiry, because there is nothing on our side to expire.

You can delete everything in two ways:

  1. Settings → Erase All Data inside the app. This permanently removes the name, cycle dates, compliments, saved dates, saved tips, ratings and settings. It cannot be undone.
  2. Delete the app from your iPhone. iOS removes the app's storage with it.

Note that if you use iCloud Backup, Apple may include the app's data in your device backup. That backup is held by Apple under your Apple ID and your control, not ours. You can exclude the app from backups in iOS Settings.

10. Security

Data is held in the app's private storage, protected by iOS app sandboxing and device encryption.

App lock. Because this app holds information about someone else, you can require Face ID, Touch ID or your device passcode every time the app opens. Turn it on in Settings → Require Face ID. While the lock is on, the app also hides its contents when it leaves the screen, so the cycle is not visible in the iOS app switcher.

Face ID and Touch ID are handled entirely by iOS. The app never receives your face or fingerprint data — only a yes-or-no answer from the system about whether authentication succeeded.

We recommend turning the app lock on. Without it, anyone who picks up your unlocked phone can open the app and see her cycle.

Reminders and your lock screen. A notification that says what is happening in her cycle is readable by anyone who glances at your phone, and the app lock cannot cover it. That is why Discreet notifications is on by default: alerts show only the app name and a neutral line, and the detail appears after you unlock the app. Turning it off makes reminders more informative and more visible to other people — the trade is yours to make.

Please note that the home-screen widget, if you add one, shows the current cycle phase on your home screen and is visible to anyone who can see your screen. The app lock does not cover it, because widgets are drawn by iOS outside the app. If that matters to you, do not add the widget.
