Privacy Policy for Trackpack

Last updated: 25 November 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Trackpack (the "App") handles information. The App is provided by Anton Razguliaev, Individual Entrepreneur ("we", "us", "our"), registered in the Republic of Armenia (state registration number 286.1580989, taxpayer ID (TIN) 20286933).

Short version: we do not run servers, accounts, analytics, advertising or tracking. We never receive your music library, your passwords or anything you do in the App. What leaves your device goes straight from your device to the music services you choose to connect, to public music catalogs (song and artist names only, to find songs), to Apple, and to your own iCloud.

1. Information we do not collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or phone number.
  • We have no servers that receive or store your data, and no analytics or advertising SDKs are in the App.
  • We do not track you across apps or websites, and the App does not use the advertising identifier.
  • We do not sell or share your data.

2. Music services you connect

To read or change your library on a service (for example Spotify, Yandex Music or YouTube Music), you sign in on that service's own website inside the App. Your password goes to the service, not to us. The App keeps the resulting sign-in session in the device Keychain and uses it, from your device, only to read your playlists, liked songs, albums and artists, and — when you start a transfer — to add songs and playlists there. You can sign out at any time in Settings → Accounts, which deletes the session. Each service handles your data under its own privacy policy.

Apple Music is used through Apple's MusicKit with your permission: to read and play your library, search the Apple Music catalog, add songs and playlists, and show personal recommendations from Apple.

3. Finding songs

To match songs between services, the App sends song titles, artist and album names and ISRC codes to public music catalogs — Apple's catalog, MusicBrainz, Deezer's public search and ListenBrainz (similar artists). These requests carry no identifiers about you.

4. Your collection and iCloud

Your collection, transfer history, settings and the covers you choose from Photos are stored on your device. If iCloud is on, your collection is synced through your own private iCloud database (CloudKit), which only you can access; we cannot. Backups you export are saved where you choose in Files.

5. On-device features

Search can use Apple Intelligence on your device to understand what you typed. This runs on the device; nothing is sent to us.

6. Purchases

Purchases are processed by Apple. We do not see your payment details. The App checks with Apple which purchases your Apple ID owns.

7. App configuration

The App downloads a signed configuration file from GitHub that switches service connections on or off. Nothing about you is sent with this request beyond what any web request carries (such as your IP address, seen by GitHub).

8. Your own music server

If you connect your own server (Navidrome, Jellyfin or Plex), the App talks to it directly on your network with the credentials you enter; they are kept in the Keychain.

9. Children

The App is not directed to children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from children.

10. Your rights

Because we hold no data about you, there is nothing for us to access, correct or delete. You control your data in the App: sign out of services, delete transfers and your collection, or delete the App. Data held by a music service is managed with that service.

11. Changes

We may update this policy; the date above shows the latest version.
