What Trackpack does

Trackpack moves your music between streaming services — playlists, liked songs and saved albums — and checks every song before it is added. It keeps your own collection of the music you love, plays it through Apple Music, and shows new releases of the artists you follow.

Trackpack is not affiliated with, endorsed or sponsored by Apple, Spotify, Yandex or any other music service. All trademarks belong to their owners.

Support

How do I restore a purchase?
Open Settings in Trackpack and tap Restore Purchases. Purchases are tied to your Apple ID; Unlock Everything is shared with your Family Sharing group.
What is the Move Pass?
14 days of unlimited transfers for one payment. It does not renew. If you then decide to keep everything forever, you pay only the difference to Unlock Everything.
How do I get a refund?
Purchases are processed by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
A song was matched wrongly or not found.
Open the transfer, tap the song and pick another version. If a service has changed and stopped working, write to us — tell us the service and what you saw.
How do I sign out of a service?
In Trackpack, open Settings → Accounts and sign out. Trackpack forgets that service's session at once.
