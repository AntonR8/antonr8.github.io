3. Sign in with Apple

The App offers Sign in with Apple. You can also use the App without signing in at all.

If you choose to sign in, Apple gives the App:

  • a user identifier — a value created by Apple specifically for this App;
  • your name, if you agree to share it;
  • your email address, or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your real one.

The App stores these values on your device only, in the app's local preferences. They are not sent to us and we never see them. We do not operate an account server; signing in does not create an account with us.

To sign out, open Settings in the App and choose "Sign out". To remove your data, use "Delete account" in the same screen: this deletes your projects and content from the device and signs you out. You can also revoke the App's access entirely in iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

5. Speech recognition — important

Dictation uses Apple's speech recognition framework built into iOS. Depending on your device, your iOS version and the selected language, the audio may be processed on Apple's servers rather than entirely on your device.

This means that when you use dictation, what you say may be transmitted to Apple for recognition. This processing is carried out by Apple under Apple's privacy policy (apple.com/legal/privacy), not by us. We do not receive your audio, we do not store it, and we have no access to it.

If you prefer not to send audio to Apple, do not use the dictation feature — type your text instead. Everything else in the App works without it.

6. Purchases and subscriptions

Paid subscriptions are sold by Apple through your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details. We never receive your card number, billing address, or Apple Account identity.

We receive from Apple only aggregated, anonymised sales and financial reports — for example, how many subscriptions were sold in a country. These reports do not identify individual buyers.

The App checks whether a valid subscription exists so it can unlock paid features. That check is made against Apple's services; it does not send us anything.

7. What we do not do

To be explicit, the App contains:

  • no analytics or telemetry of any kind;
  • no advertising and no advertising identifiers;
  • no tracking across apps or websites, and no data shared with data brokers;
  • no third-party SDKs — the App has zero third-party dependencies;
  • no crash reporting service.

Because of this, the App's App Store privacy label is "Data Not Collected".

10. Data retention and deletion

We do not retain your data, because we do not receive it.

On your device:

  • Delete a project or post — removes that content from the App;
  • Delete account in the App's Settings — removes your projects and content and signs you out;
  • Delete the App — removes the App's local storage, including your entire content plan.

Deleting the App does not cancel a paid subscription. Manage that in Settings → your name → Subscriptions.

11. Children

The App is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect any data from anyone. If you believe a child has provided us with personal data, contact us and we will look into it.

12. International transfers

Because your data stays on your device, the App itself does not transfer your personal data across borders.

Two exceptions are handled by Apple rather than by us: speech recognition (section 5) and payments (section 6). Apple operates globally and its transfers are governed by Apple's own privacy policy and safeguards.

13. Security

Your data is protected by the security of your own device: the App relies on iOS sandboxing and the device's encryption. Keep a passcode or biometric lock enabled — that is the single most effective protection for your content plan.

Because nothing is sent to a server, there is no server-side database of user content that could be breached.
