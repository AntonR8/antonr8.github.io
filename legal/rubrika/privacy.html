Rubrika — content plan calendar for iOS

Effective date: 3 August 2026

Short version

Your content plan — projects, posts, texts and photos — lives on your device and, if you keep iCloud sync on, in your own private iCloud. We have no server for your content and cannot read it.

The App does send a limited amount of data outside your device, and this policy lists all of it:

  • Apple — iCloud sync and shared projects, payments, and speech recognition for dictation;
  • Adapty — our subscription service: your purchases and subscription status, under an anonymous profile;
  • TelemetryDeck — privacy-focused, anonymous usage analytics: which features are used, never what you write.

There is no advertising in the App, no tracking across apps or websites, and we never sell your data. None of the data we receive is linked to your name, email or Apple Account.

Shared projects. If you invite other people to a project, Apple shares that project with them through iCloud sharing. The people you invite can see and edit the project's content, and you can see their names as Apple shows them. You control who is invited and can stop sharing at any time.

If you have iCloud Backup or a computer backup switched on, your device backup may also include the App's data. iCloud and backups are governed by Apple's privacy policy.

3. Sign in with Apple

The App offers Sign in with Apple. You can also use the App without signing in at all.

If you choose to sign in, Apple gives the App:

  • a user identifier — a value created by Apple specifically for this App;
  • your name, if you agree to share it;
  • your email address, or Apple's private relay address if you choose to hide your real one.

The App stores these values on your device only, in the app's local preferences. They are not sent to us, to Adapty or to TelemetryDeck. We do not operate an account server; signing in does not create an account with us.

To sign out, open Settings in the App and choose "Sign out". To remove your data, use "Delete account" in the same screen: this deletes your projects and content (from iCloud too, if sync is on) and your sign-in details, and signs you out. You can also revoke the App's access in iOS Settings → your name → Sign-In & Security → Sign in with Apple.

5. Speech recognition — important

Dictation uses Apple's speech recognition framework built into iOS. Depending on your device, your iOS version and the selected language, the audio may be processed on Apple's servers rather than entirely on your device.

This processing is carried out by Apple under Apple's privacy policy, not by us. We do not receive your audio, we do not store it, and we have no access to it. If you prefer not to send audio to Apple, type your text instead — everything else in the App works without dictation.

6. Purchases and subscriptions (Adapty)

Paid subscriptions are sold by Apple through your Apple Account. Apple processes the payment and holds your payment details. We never receive your card number, billing address, or Apple Account identity.

To show prices, unlock paid features and keep subscription status correct, the App uses Adapty (Adapty Tech Inc., adapty.io/privacy). Adapty creates an anonymous profile for your installation — it does not contain your name, email or Apple Account. Adapty processes:

  • purchase history and subscription status — products, transaction identifiers, dates, prices, trial and renewal status, as reported by Apple;
  • device and app information — device model, iOS and App version, language and region, the vendor identifier (IDFV), and your IP address, from which an approximate country is derived;
  • Apple Ads attribution — if you installed the App after tapping an ad on the App Store, Apple provides which campaign it was (through Apple's AdServices framework). This tells us whether our App Store ads work; it does not track you across other apps or websites;
  • ready-made plan usage — the identifier of the last ready-made content plan you applied and where you opened it from (for example, an App Store in-app event), so we can see which plans lead to subscriptions.

We also receive from Apple aggregated sales and financial reports that do not identify individual buyers.

7. Usage analytics (TelemetryDeck)

To understand which features are useful and where the App can be improved, we use TelemetryDeck (TelemetryDeck GmbH, Germany, telemetrydeck.com/privacy), an analytics service designed for privacy. The App sends short signals when certain things happen, for example:

  • onboarding completed; a project or a post created;
  • the subscription screen shown, a purchase or restore started, completed or failed, a free-plan limit reached;
  • a ready-made content plan opened or applied;
  • the rating prompt shown and the rating selected.

Each signal carries technical context — App version, iOS version, device model, language and region — and an anonymous identifier: the TelemetryDeck SDK hashes the vendor identifier (IDFV) on your device, and TelemetryDeck hashes it again with a salt on receipt, so the original value never leaves your device and cannot be traced back to you. Signals never contain your content: no post texts, project names, photos, names or email addresses. TelemetryDeck does not store IP addresses with the signals. We only look at aggregated statistics.

8. Other network requests

The App downloads a small configuration file from our website (hosted on GitHub Pages) to switch features on or off without an update. Like any web request, it reveals your IP address to the hosting provider; the request contains no data about you.

15. Security

Your content is protected by your device's encryption and sandboxing and, when synced, by Apple's iCloud security. Keep a passcode or biometric lock enabled — that is the single most effective protection for your content plan. Because your content never reaches our servers, there is no developer-side database of user content that could be breached.
